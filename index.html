About

Bridging HR Strategy
with Organizational Reality

Having worked across India, Dubai, Thailand, and South Africa, Dr. Krishan Singh brings a rare global perspective to HR advisory — while remaining deeply rooted in the realities of Indian industry, manufacturing, and labour relations.

As Senior Vice President – HR at SRF Limited, he has been an integral member of the business leadership team, responsible for driving people strategy across seven manufacturing plants, the R&D Centre, and Pan-India Sales operations. His expertise spans the full spectrum of human resources — from industrial relations governance to culture transformation.

Prior to SRF, Dr. Krishan has led HR functions across engineering, chemicals, sugar, FMCG, consumer products, and a German MNC — giving him a multi-sector lens on organizational challenges.
